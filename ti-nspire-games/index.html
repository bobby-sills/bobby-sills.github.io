ti-nspire games

here are ten games to play on your calculator while bored in math class. there is no need to jailbreak anything, as everything is written in the native ti-nspire lua code. these are for the ti-nspire cx ii specifically and they need os 3.0.2 or newer.

installation instructions:

  1. click the download link next to the game you would like to install
  2. open https://nspireconnect.ti.com/ (note: this only works on chromium browsers)
  3. connect your calculator to your computer with its included usb cable
  4. click "transfer file", and then click "from your computer"
  5. click the game(s) you would like to transfer and voila!

or download all ten games at once as a zip.
