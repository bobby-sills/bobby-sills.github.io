ambulance taxi laos

a clickable demo of two apps for calling and running ambulances in laos, like ride-hailing but for emergencies. pick one to try.

patient app

request an ambulance in a few taps, watch it arrive, pay afterwards. english and lao.

crew app

take the call, drive to the patient, record their condition and hand over at the er.
