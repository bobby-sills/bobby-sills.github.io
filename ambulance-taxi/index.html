ambulance taxi laos

a clickable demo of two apps for calling and running ambulances in laos, like ride-hailing but for emergencies.

patient app

request an ambulance in a few taps, watch it arrive, pay afterwards. english and lao. the dark “demo” buttons skip the waiting.

open full screen

crew app

go on duty and a call rings after a few seconds. accept it, drive to the patient, record their condition and hand over at the er.
