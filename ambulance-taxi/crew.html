crew app

for the ambulance driver or paramedic: one job at a time, from the call to handover at the hospital.

  1. the app starts on duty. a call rings after a few seconds.
  2. accept it and tap “arrived at scene”.
  3. set each patient’s condition, pick a hospital, then load up and hand over.
